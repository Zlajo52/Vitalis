3D Animation

We started with character rigging in Blender, using Rigify to build a working bone structure for a simple fox model. It was our first real look at how a static 3D character gets a skeleton that can actually be posed and animated.

3D Animation pt2

In part two we took a fully rigged knight character further — posing it, lighting the scene, and then compositing the render into real video footage of a city at night. That meant learning Blender's node-based compositor and video sequencer alongside the 3D work, to key, layer, and blend the render into the live-action shot.

Digital Business

We designed and built a small business database in FileMaker, modeling contacts, products, and orders for a practice auto-repair shop. Beyond the data itself we also built the layouts by hand — the product catalog, the order form, and the invoice — so the system could generate a real, print-ready invoice from an order.
